photonicslab.visualize.VisualizeBeam2D.add_filter_circular¶

VisualizeBeam2D.add_filter_circular(center_x=0.0, center_y=0.0, inner_r=0.0, outer_r=0.05, filter_nd=100.0)[source]¶

Adds a circular obstruction to the incident beam.

Parameters

  • center_x (float) – X co-ordinate of the center of the heat source [cm].

  • center_y (float) – Y co-ordinate of the center of the heat source [cm].

  • inner_r (float) – Radius from the center to inner section of the heat source [cm].

  • outer_r (float) – Radius from the center to outer section of the heat source [cm].

Raises

ValueError – If the coordinates of the position of the heat source are out of bounds of crystal face.

pts¶

Cross sectional coordinates for which to add obstruction.

Type

ndarray
